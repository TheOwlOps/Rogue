## Original engine — Jérémy Perret

The avatar engine at the heart of `@theowlops/rogue-avatar-core`
(`packages/avatar-core/src/bot/`) is derived from an SVG avatar engine by
**Jérémy Perret**, a recreation of the x.ai bot avatar, used and extended
under the MIT license (see `LICENSE`). The Rogue expansion pack (+100 catalogue entries: 52 states,
12 shapes, 12 expressions, 24 colours) and the SDK layer (renderer, player,
chat phases, React/Vue wrappers) were added by TheOwlOps.
